Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what Kervan collects, why, and what you can do about it. Kervan is used by people who share a house, so most of what you add is visible to the people in your house and to your organization's coordinators.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: the name, photo, and language you choose, your house, and your role (for example, mentor or ev abi).
  • What you add in the app: shopping lists and receipts, duty posts and photos, ratings, meeting replies, house visits, announcements, and your weekly schedule if you enter it.
  • Location: only if you allow it. Your location is shared with the people in your house and your coordinators so the house can see who is home. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send you push notifications, and your notification settings.

How we use it

Only to run Kervan: to show your house its lists, duties, meetings, and calendar, and to send you notifications about them. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • People in your house see what you share in the house.
  • Your organization's coordinators can see the houses in their organization, including members, scores, and shared locations.
  • Ratings of meals and cleaning are anonymous to other house members.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign in.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications.
  • Apple and Google sign in, if you choose them.
  • Open Food Facts receives the words you search for when you look up a product. It does not receive who you are.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Delete account. This removes your account, profile, photos, posts, ratings, location, and schedule right away. Shared house lists you started (for example, a shopping list or a visit on the house calendar) stay for your house, without your name.

If you can't open the app, email mysahin08@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Children

Kervan is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and let you know in the app when the change matters.
